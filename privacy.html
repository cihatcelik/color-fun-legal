Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · App: Color Fun: Kids Coloring (com.elapps.kidscoloringapp) · Developer: ELApps

The short version: Color Fun is made for children. We do not ask for names, emails, photos, location or any other personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Drawings stay on the device. Optional VIP purchases are handled by Apple or Google, and managed with an anonymous ID by our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

1. Who we are

Color Fun is developed by ELApps (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at elappstest@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the app does not ask for:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account/login information;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • contacts, camera, microphone or the contents of your photo library;
  • advertising identifiers (IDFA / Advertising ID), analytics or behavioural data.

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no social features, no chat and no links that children can follow without passing a parent check.

3. Information stored only on the device

To let children continue their pictures later, the app stores which colors were used in each drawing and when it was last edited. This data is saved in a local database on the device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a drawing in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes this data.

4. Photos permission

If you choose Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add the finished picture to your photo library. This permission is requested only at that moment, is used only to save the picture, and the app does not read, upload or scan your existing photos. You can decline or revoke it at any time in your device settings; coloring keeps working without it.

5. Optional purchases (VIP)

VIP is an optional subscription that a parent can buy behind a parent check. Payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see or receive card or payment details.

To check whether VIP is active and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID (not linked to a name or email), purchase and transaction information from the store (such as product, price, currency, purchase and expiry dates), and basic technical information needed to provide the service (such as app version, device/OS type, country/storefront and IP address used to make the request). This information is used only to provide and verify purchases. RevenueCat’s privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you never open the parent area or make a purchase, the app still contacts RevenueCat at start-up to check entitlement status using the anonymous ID; no personal information is sent.

6. Children’s privacy

Color Fun is directed to children. We comply with the principles of the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR provisions for children, Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), and the Apple App Store Kids category and Google Play Families policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Purchases, links outside the app and settings for grown-ups are placed behind a parent check.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not use data for advertising or profiling. The only third parties involved are Apple/Google (to process payments) and RevenueCat (to manage subscriptions), as described above.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Drawings: kept on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple/Google and RevenueCat as required to provide the subscription and for legal/accounting obligations. Because the RevenueCat ID is anonymous, we cannot link it to you; to request deletion, contact us with the approximate purchase date and store, and we will work with RevenueCat to delete the associated records where possible.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Since we do not hold personal data that identifies you, most requests can be satisfied by deleting the app. For anything else, contact elappstest@gmail.com; we will reply within 30 days.

10. Security

Data on the device is protected by the operating system’s app sandbox. Communication with RevenueCat and the app stores uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and, for material changes, inform users through the app store listing or the app itself before the change applies.
