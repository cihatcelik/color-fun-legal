How do I open the parent area?

Tap VIP on the home screen (or a locked VIP picture), then Parent area. Answer the multiplication question written in words to continue.

How do I restore my VIP purchase?

Open the parent area and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account used for the purchase.

How do I cancel the subscription?

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Color Fun → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Color Fun → Cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Where are my drawings saved?

Drawings are saved automatically on your device and appear in My Drawings. They are not uploaded anywhere, so uninstalling the app or switching devices removes them. To keep a picture, use Save picture → Save to Photos.

“Save to Photos” doesn’t work

The app needs permission to add pictures to your photo library. If you declined it, enable it in device Settings → Color Fun → Photos (iPhone) or Settings → Apps → Color Fun → Permissions (Android).

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Coloring, saving and My Drawings all work offline. An internet connection is only needed for VIP purchases and restoring them.
